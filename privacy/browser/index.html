Privacy policy

Curmudgeon Works · applies to Curmudgeon Browser · last updated 14 October 2026

The short version

Curmudgeon Browser shows the websites you open. We do not collect anything about you: no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, no crash reporting. What you browse stays on your device.

What goes over the network

A browser has to talk to the sites you visit, and those sites see what any site sees: your IP address, the pages you request, and the cookies they set. Their own privacy policies apply. Apart from that, the app contacts:

  • Your search engine · DuckDuckGo by default, or the one you pick in Settings, when you search from the address bar.
  • Google search suggestions · off by default. If you switch them on, what you type in the address bar is sent to Google to get suggestions. Addresses you visit are not sent.
  • Block list · off by default. If you switch it on, the list is downloaded from the address shown in Settings (StevenBlack hosts list on GitHub by default) and checked on your device. Nothing is sent back.
  • Safe Browsing · on by default, provided by Android's WebView (Google). It checks pages against lists of known harmful sites, under Google's own policies. You can switch it off in Settings.

No data ever goes to Curmudgeon Works.

What stays on your device

Your history, bookmarks, open tabs, cookies, site data, per-site settings and downloads. They live in the app's private storage (downloads go to your Downloads folder). The app opts out of Android's cloud backup. You can clear any of it in Settings → Data, or have it cleared when you exit.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state · to load web pages.
  • Location, camera and microphone · only when a website asks for them, and only after you allow it for that site. Location is off by default. The app itself never uses them.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) · to save downloads.

Third parties

Besides the sites and services listed above, which you choose to use, no third party receives data from the app. Google Play may collect installation and crash statistics under Google's own policies, which are outside our control.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the new text will be published here. Questions: hello@curmudgeon.works.
